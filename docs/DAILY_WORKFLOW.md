# Suggested study planning workflow

1. Create course records with course code, term, instructor, and overview.
2. Add each assignment or project inside its course with a deadline and effort estimate. These items appear in the planner automatically.
3. Attach study materials or save links and notes to the matching course.
4. Add class times or other academic commitments in the Study Plan tab.
5. Build today's study plan and schedule study blocks around your commitments.
6. Review deadline risk and weekly progress in the Progress tab.
7. Save general concepts and reminders in Study Notes; mark completed coursework as you finish it.
8. If Google is connected, sync changes to Calendar. Gmail reminders can email approaching deadlines while the dashboard is open.

The planner ranks deadlines and priorities and estimates how much work fits the available study budget. Review and adjust the suggested plan to match your actual workload.
